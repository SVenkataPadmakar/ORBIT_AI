## 2. Complete Voice-Over Narration Script

```text
[00:00 - 00:30] CHAPTER 01 — INTRODUCTION
"What if a startup could be built with an AI founding team?
Welcome to Orbit, the AI Founder Operating System transforming ideas into real, shipping products.
Not a generic chatbot, not a collection of templates, but fifteen autonomous specialist agents orchestrating your company from raw concept to deployment."

[00:30 - 01:15] CHAPTER 02 — CREATING STARTUP MISSION
"We initiate our mission with a single vision: an AI Interview Preparation Platform providing real-time behavioral and technical mock interviews with automated rubric feedback.
Orbit initializes mission control, allocating persistent project memory, compiling the LangGraph consensus pipeline, and activating the autonomous agent council."

[01:15 - 01:45] CHAPTER 03 — PROBLEM DISCOVERY
"The Problem Discovery Agent activates, analyzing candidate friction, acute performance anxiety, and unvalidated market assumptions.
Correlating real developer feedback, Orbit isolates the core wedge: eighty percent of candidates lack actionable verbal rubric feedback during technical screens."

[01:45 - 02:30] CHAPTER 04 — MARKET RESEARCH
"Market Research calculates our total addressable market at eighteen point four billion dollars, with a six hundred and fifty million dollar initial obtainable market.
Benchmarking existing incumbents reveals our defensible moat: sub-one-hundred-and-twenty-millisecond conversational speech reasoning that legacy platforms cannot match."

[02:30 - 03:15] CHAPTER 05 — PRODUCT STRATEGY
"Product Strategy translates research into decisive MVP boundaries, user personas, and a ruthless eighty-twenty feature priority list.
Expensive video avatar synthesis is deprioritized to preserve runway, locking in four core MVP features that maximize candidate retention."

[03:15 - 04:00] CHAPTER 06 — UI/UX ARCHITECT
"The UI and UX Architect synthesizes our design system: sixty-four deep space design tokens, interactive waveform wireframes, and high-fidelity candidate dashboards.
Every interface component adheres strictly to WCAG two point one double-A accessibility standards."

[04:00 - 04:45] CHAPTER 07 — TECHNICAL ARCHITECT
"The Technical Architect formulates production-grade PostgreSQL relational schemas, OpenAPI three point one contracts, and an asynchronous WebSockets topology.
Indexes, foreign keys, and query budgets are verified with zero syntax errors before a single line of application code is written."

[04:45 - 05:15] CHAPTER 08 — SPRINT PLANNER
"Sprint Planner turns architectural specifications into two-week agile Kanban sprints.
Eighteen prioritized user stories are staged with comprehensive acceptance criteria, test cases, and a rigorous definition of done."

[05:15 - 06:00] CHAPTER 09 — GEMINI CO-FOUNDER
"The founder asks: What should I build first?

[06:00 - 06:45] CHAPTER 10 — STARTUP BLUEPRINT
"At the center of Orbit, all fifteen specialist outputs converge.
The PRD, database schemas, API specs, sprint backlog, and financial models unite into one living, investor-ready Startup Blueprint."

[06:45 - 07:15] CHAPTER 11 — LAUNCH CENTER
"Launch Center completes automated pre-flight verification.
Security headers, SSL certificates, zero-day rollback triggers, and telemetry are verified. Ninety-four percent launch readiness achieved."

[07:15 - 07:30] CHAPTER 12 — CLOSING
"From Idea To Product.
Powered By Orbit."
```

## 4. Recording Checklist

- [ ] **Display Setup**: 3840×2160 (4K) or 1920×1080 (Full HD) at 100% OS scaling (no DPI fractional scaling).
- [ ] **Frame Rate**: Strictly 60.0 FPS recording (OBS Studio / Screenflow / Camtasia).
- [ ] **Browser Window**: Chrome or Arc in full-screen mode (`F11`), bookmarks bar hidden, developer extensions disabled.
- [ ] **Cursor**: Hardware cursor enabled with 1.25x size scaling for high readability; mouse speed set to moderate damping.
- [ ] **Audio Capture**: 24-bit 48kHz WAV audio; voiceover leveled at -14 LUFS integrated loudness with mild compression and de-essing.
- [ ] **Demo Seed State**: Ensure deterministic demo data seed is loaded (`ai-interview-prep` mission loaded).
- [ ] **Lighting & Ambient**: Cosmic black background (#030308) verified across all routes without browser white flashes during page transitions.
